# Question 6
## What are the differences between 802.11a and 802.11b?

# Answer

802.11a and 802.11b are early Wi‑Fi standards introduced by IEEE with major differences in frequency bands, modulation, and performance.

---

# Comparison Table

| Feature | 802.11a | 802.11b |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency Band | 5 GHz | 2.4 GHz |
| Maximum Data Rate | 54 Mbps | 11 Mbps |
| Modulation | OFDM | DSSS |
| Range | Shorter | Longer |
| Interference | Lower | Higher |
| Channel Availability | More channels | Fewer channels |

---

# 802.11a Characteristics

- Uses 5 GHz band
- Higher throughput
- Less interference
- Shorter coverage due to higher frequency

---

# 802.11b Characteristics

- Uses 2.4 GHz band
- Better range
- Lower throughput
- More interference from Bluetooth and microwave devices

---

# Conclusion

802.11a provided higher speeds with lower interference, while 802.11b offered better range but lower throughput.
