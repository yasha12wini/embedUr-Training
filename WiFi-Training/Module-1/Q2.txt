3. Matching to Wi-Fi Generations
Based on the properties observed, the device corresponds to Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax).
Although the hardware (AX211) is Wi-Fi 6E capable, the current operational state is defined as follows:

Generation Match: Wi-Fi 6 (HE - High Efficiency)
Standard: IEEE 802.11ax
Reasoning:
Modulation: The 1201 Mbps rate indicates the use of 1024-QAM (MCS 11), a hallmark of Wi-Fi 6.Efficiency: The jump to 1201 Mbps confirms an 80 MHz channel width with 2 Spatial Streams (2x2 MIMO).
In the 2.4 GHz scenario, the rate of 287 Mbps confirms a 20 MHz channel width, which is the maximum efficiency for 802.11ax on that narrow band.

Security: The use of WPA3-Personal (SAE) aligns with the mandatory security requirements introduced alongside the Wi-Fi 6 certification.

While the Wi-Fi 6 standard theoretically supports up to 9.6 Gbps, this specific connection is limited to 1.2 Gbps (1201 Mbps).This is due to the Physical Layer constraints like Channel Width: The connection uses 80 MHz instead of the maximum 160 MHz.
MIMO Configuration: The link uses 2x2 MIMO (standard for mobile/laptop) instead of the theoretical 8x8 MIMO.
Frequency Band: While the adapter supports the 6 GHz band (Wi-Fi 6E), the Access Point (Hotspot) is currently restricted to the 5 GHz band.
