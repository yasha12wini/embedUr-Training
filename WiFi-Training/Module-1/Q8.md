# Question 8
## What is the difference between IEEE and WFA?

# Answer

IEEE and Wi‑Fi Alliance (WFA) are two important organizations involved in Wi‑Fi technology, but they perform different roles.

---

# IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)

IEEE develops wireless communication standards.

Example:
- IEEE 802.11 standards

---

# IEEE Responsibilities

- Defines technical specifications
- Creates PHY and MAC standards
- Standardizes wireless communication protocols

---

# Wi‑Fi Alliance (WFA)

The Wi‑Fi Alliance is an industry organization responsible for:
- Certification
- Interoperability testing
- Branding

---

# WFA Responsibilities

- Wi‑Fi certification
- Device interoperability validation
- Security certification
- Wi‑Fi branding programs

---

# Comparison Table

| Feature | IEEE | WFA |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Standards development | Certification |
| Focus | Technical protocol definition | Interoperability |
| Example | 802.11ax standard | Wi‑Fi 6 certification |
| Type | Engineering standards body | Industry alliance |

---

# Conclusion

IEEE creates Wi‑Fi technical standards, while the Wi‑Fi Alliance ensures devices correctly implement and interoperate with those standards.
