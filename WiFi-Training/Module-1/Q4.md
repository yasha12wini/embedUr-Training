# Question 4
## What are the basic functionalities of a Wi‑Fi Access Point?

# Answer

A Wi‑Fi Access Point (AP) is a networking device that enables wireless devices to connect to a wired network using IEEE 802.11 standards.

---

# Basic Functions of an Access Point

## 1. Wireless Connectivity
Provides wireless access for:
- Laptops
- Smartphones
- Tablets
- IoT devices

---

## 2. Frame Transmission
Handles wireless frame exchange between:
- Clients
- Wired LAN

---

## 3. Authentication
Verifies user identity using:
- WPA2/WPA3
- 802.1X
- PSK authentication

---

## 4. Encryption
Secures wireless communication using:
- AES
- TKIP
- WPA security mechanisms

---

## 5. Beacon Transmission
Periodically broadcasts:
- SSID
- Channel information
- Supported capabilities

---

## 6. Traffic Bridging
Bridges wireless traffic to Ethernet networks.

---

## 7. RF Management
Controls:
- Channel selection
- Transmit power
- Interference mitigation

---

# Conclusion

Access Points are the core infrastructure devices of WLANs, enabling wireless connectivity, security, traffic forwarding, and network management.
