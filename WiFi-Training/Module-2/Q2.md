# Question 2
## Describe CAPWAP and explain the flow between AP and Controller

# Answer

CAPWAP (Control And Provisioning of Wireless Access Points) is a protocol used for communication between lightweight APs and Wireless LAN Controllers (WLCs).

It standardizes AP management, control signaling, and data tunneling.

---

# Purpose of CAPWAP

CAPWAP enables:
- Centralized AP management
- AP configuration
- Firmware updates
- Security policy enforcement
- WLAN management

---

# CAPWAP Communication Flow

## Step 1 — AP Bootup
The lightweight AP powers on and initializes hardware.

---

## Step 2 — IP Address Acquisition
AP obtains an IP address using:
- DHCP
- Static IP configuration

---

## Step 3 — WLC Discovery
AP discovers the controller using:
- DHCP option 43
- DNS lookup
- Broadcast
- Static configuration

---

## Step 4 — CAPWAP Tunnel Establishment
AP establishes:
- Control tunnel
- Data tunnel

with the WLC.

---

## Step 5 — Authentication
Mutual authentication occurs using DTLS certificates.

---

## Step 6 — Configuration Download
WLC sends:
- WLAN profiles
- SSIDs
- Security settings
- RF parameters

---

## Step 7 — AP Joins WLC
AP becomes operational and starts serving wireless clients.

---

# Advantages of CAPWAP

- Vendor interoperability
- Centralized WLAN management
- Simplified AP deployment
- Secure AP‑controller communication
- Scalable enterprise WLAN architecture

# Conclusion

CAPWAP is the backbone protocol for lightweight enterprise WLAN deployments, enabling centralized management and efficient AP‑controller communication.
