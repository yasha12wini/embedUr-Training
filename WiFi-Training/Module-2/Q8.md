# Question 8
## What are the challenges of deploying more than 50 autonomous APs in a large network like a university?

# Answer

Deploying large numbers of autonomous APs creates major operational and scalability challenges in enterprise environments.

---

# Major Challenges

## 1. Manual Configuration
Each AP must be configured individually.

Problems:
- Time consuming
- Human errors
- Inconsistent configurations

---

## 2. Difficult RF Management
Autonomous APs cannot coordinate automatically.

Challenges:
- Channel interference
- Overlapping coverage
- Poor roaming optimization

---

## 3. Security Management Complexity
Security policies must be manually updated across all APs.

Problems:
- Inconsistent security
- Weak compliance management

---

## 4. Poor Scalability
Managing 50+ APs individually becomes operationally inefficient.

---

## 5. Firmware Upgrade Difficulty
Firmware updates require:
- Manual upgrades
- Scheduled maintenance
- Device‑by‑device management

---

## 6. Roaming Problems
Without centralized coordination:
- Roaming becomes slower
- Clients may disconnect
- Handoff delays increase

---

## 7. Monitoring Limitations
No centralized dashboard exists for:
- Client monitoring
- RF analysis
- Fault detection

---

# Autonomous AP Deployment Issues

| Issue | Impact |
|---|---|
| Manual management | High operational overhead |
| No centralized RF optimization | Increased interference |
| Weak roaming coordination | Poor user experience |
| Difficult troubleshooting | Increased downtime |
| Inconsistent policies | Security risks |

---

# Conclusion

Large enterprise deployments require centralized WLAN architectures because autonomous AP deployments become difficult to manage, secure, and scale efficiently.
