# Multi-Link Operation (MLO) and Its Impact on Throughput and Latency

## Introduction

Multi-Link Operation (MLO) is one of the most important innovations introduced in Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be).

MLO allows devices to:
- Simultaneously transmit and receive data across multiple frequency bands or channels.

This fundamentally improves:
- Throughput
- Latency
- Reliability

---

# Traditional Wi-Fi Limitation

In earlier Wi-Fi generations:
- A client typically communicates using a single link at a time.

Example:
- Either 5 GHz OR 6 GHz

Problems:
- Congestion on one band affects performance
- Failover delays occur
- Limited scheduling flexibility

---

# What is MLO?

MLO allows a device to establish:
- Multiple concurrent links between client and AP.

Example:
- 5 GHz + 6 GHz simultaneously

These links can:
- Carry traffic together
- Balance traffic dynamically

---

# How MLO Works

## Multi-Link Device (MLD)

Wi-Fi 7 devices supporting MLO are called:
- Multi-Link Devices

An MLD manages:
- Multiple radios
- Multiple channels
- Coordinated transmissions

---

# Types of MLO Operation

## 1. Simultaneous Transmission
Traffic flows across multiple links together.

## 2. Dynamic Link Switching
Traffic shifts based on:
- Congestion
- Interference
- Channel quality

---

# Impact on Throughput

## Increased Aggregate Bandwidth

Multiple links combine bandwidth.

Example:
- 5 GHz throughput + 6 GHz throughput

Result:
- Much higher effective throughput

---

## Better Channel Utilization

Traffic can be distributed intelligently across links.

Benefits:
- Reduced bottlenecks
- Better efficiency

---

# Impact on Latency

## Lower Delay

Traffic can avoid congested channels.

Benefits:
- Faster packet delivery
- Reduced queueing delay

---

## Faster Retransmissions

If one link experiences issues:
- Another link can continue communication

This improves:
- Reliability
- Responsiveness

---

# Reliability Improvements

MLO improves resilience against:
- Interference
- Congestion
- Channel fading

Multiple links provide redundancy.

---

# Real-World Applications

## Gaming
Low latency improves responsiveness.

## AR/VR
Stable high-throughput links reduce lag.

## Industrial Automation
Reliable low-latency communication becomes possible.

---

# MLO vs Traditional Roaming

Traditional roaming:
- Switches completely between bands/APs

MLO:
- Uses multiple links simultaneously

This creates smoother connectivity.

---

# Enterprise Importance

MLO is valuable for:
- Dense deployments
- Real-time applications
- High-capacity WLANs

It is a major architectural advancement in Wi-Fi.

---

# Key Takeaways

- MLO allows simultaneous multi-band communication.
- It improves throughput by aggregating bandwidth.
- It reduces latency through intelligent link usage.
- MLO significantly improves reliability and efficiency in Wi-Fi 7.
