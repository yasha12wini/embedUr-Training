
# Types of PPDU and PPDU Frame Formats Across Wi‑Fi Generations

## Introduction

PPDU (PLCP Protocol Data Unit) is the PHY layer frame transmitted over the wireless medium in IEEE 802.11 WLAN systems.

Different Wi‑Fi generations introduced different PPDU formats to support:
- Higher throughput
- MIMO
- OFDMA
- Beamforming
- Multi‑user transmission

---

## Major Types of PPDU

| Wi‑Fi Generation | PPDU Type |
|---|---|
| 802.11a/g | Legacy PPDU |
| 802.11n | HT PPDU |
| 802.11ac | VHT PPDU |
| 802.11ax | HE PPDU |
| 802.11be | EHT PPDU |

---

## 1. Legacy PPDU (802.11a/g)

Structure:
- Legacy Preamble
- SIGNAL field
- Data field

Features:
- Single user
- OFDM transmission
- Up to 64‑QAM

---

## Legacy Preamble Components

Components:
- Short Training Field (STF)
- Long Training Field (LTF)
- SIGNAL field

Purpose:
- Synchronization
- Channel estimation
- Rate signaling

---

## 2. HT PPDU (802.11n)

HT = High Throughput

Enhancements:
- MIMO support
- 40 MHz channels
- Spatial streams

Components:
- Legacy preamble
- HT‑SIG
- HT‑STF
- HT‑LTF
- Data

---

## HT Mixed Mode

Most common format:
- Maintains backward compatibility
- Legacy devices can detect frames

---

## 3. VHT PPDU (802.11ac)

VHT = Very High Throughput

Features:
- 80/160 MHz channels
- MU‑MIMO
- 256‑QAM

Components:
- Legacy fields
- VHT‑SIG‑A
- VHT‑STF
- VHT‑LTF
- VHT‑SIG‑B
- Data

---

## VHT Improvements

Adds:
- Beamforming support
- User allocation information
- Enhanced signaling

---

## 4. HE PPDU (802.11ax)

HE = High Efficiency

Supports:
- OFDMA
- MU‑MIMO
- 1024‑QAM

Components:
- Legacy preamble
- HE‑SIG‑A
- HE‑SIG‑B
- HE‑STF
- HE‑LTF
- Data

---

## HE PPDU Features

Major improvements:
- Resource Unit signaling
- Trigger‑based uplink
- Dense network optimization
- Extended range operation

---

## HE PPDU Variants

Types:
- HE SU
- HE MU
- HE ER SU
- HE TB

---

## 5. EHT PPDU (802.11be / Wi‑Fi 7)

EHT = Extremely High Throughput

Features:
- 320 MHz channels
- Multi‑Link Operation
- 4096‑QAM

Enhancements:
- Higher spatial streams
- Reduced latency
- Improved scheduling

---

## PPDU Evolution Summary

| Feature | Legacy | HT | VHT | HE | EHT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OFDM | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| MIMO | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| MU‑MIMO | No | Limited | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| OFDMA | No | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Max Modulation | 64‑QAM | 64‑QAM | 256‑QAM | 1024‑QAM | 4096‑QAM |

---

## Importance of PPDU Formats

PPDU formats enable:
- Backward compatibility
- Efficient synchronization
- Multi‑user scheduling
- Advanced PHY operation

Packet analyzers decode PPDU fields for:
- RF troubleshooting
- WLAN optimization
- Standards validation

---

## Conclusion

PPDU formats evolved significantly across Wi‑Fi generations to support advanced PHY features such as MIMO, MU‑MIMO, OFDMA, beamforming, and ultra‑high throughput operation. Understanding PPDU structures is critical for WLAN engineering, packet analysis, and enterprise wireless optimization.
