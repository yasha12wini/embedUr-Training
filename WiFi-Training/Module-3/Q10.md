
# PPDU, PLCP, and PMD in the IEEE 802.11 PHY Layer

## Introduction

The IEEE 802.11 PHY layer is divided into logical sublayers and transmission units that manage wireless communication.

Important PHY layer components:
- PPDU
- PLCP
- PMD

These elements work together to convert MAC frames into RF signals suitable for wireless transmission.

---

## PHY Layer Architecture

IEEE 802.11 PHY layer consists of:
1. PLCP (Physical Layer Convergence Procedure)
2. PMD (Physical Medium Dependent)

The output transmission unit is:
- PPDU (PLCP Protocol Data Unit)

---

## What is PLCP?

PLCP acts as the interface between:
- MAC layer
- Physical transmission layer

Responsibilities:
- Framing
- PHY header generation
- Synchronization support
- Rate information signaling

PLCP converts:
- PSDU → PPDU

---

## PLCP Functions

Main PLCP tasks:
- Add preamble
- Add PHY header
- Indicate modulation and coding scheme
- Specify packet length
- Support receiver synchronization

---

## What is PMD?

PMD handles actual RF transmission.

Responsibilities:
- Modulation
- Coding
- Frequency conversion
- Signal transmission
- Signal reception

PMD interacts directly with:
- Antennas
- RF front‑end circuitry

---

## PMD Operations

PMD performs:
- Scrambling
- FEC encoding
- Interleaving
- Symbol mapping
- OFDM processing
- DAC/ADC conversion

---

## What is PPDU?

PPDU stands for:
- PLCP Protocol Data Unit

It is the complete PHY frame transmitted over the air.

PPDU contains:
- Preamble
- PHY header
- PSDU payload

---

## PPDU Structure

Typical PPDU structure:
1. Preamble
2. Signal field
3. Data field
4. Tail bits
5. Padding

---

## Relationship Between PSDU and PPDU

MAC frame:
- Called PSDU inside PHY layer

After PLCP processing:
- PSDU becomes PPDU

---

## Workflow

Step‑by‑step process:

1. MAC generates PSDU
2. PLCP adds PHY information
3. PPDU formed
4. PMD modulates signal
5. RF transmission occurs

---

## Evolution Across Wi‑Fi Standards

### Legacy Standards
- Simple PPDU formats
- Single antenna systems

### 802.11n
- HT PPDU
- MIMO support

### 802.11ac
- VHT PPDU
- MU‑MIMO support

### 802.11ax
- HE PPDU
- OFDMA support
- Resource Unit signaling

---

## Conclusion

PLCP, PMD, and PPDU are essential building blocks of the IEEE 802.11 PHY layer. PLCP manages framing and PHY signaling, PMD handles actual modulation and RF transmission, and PPDU represents the final over‑the‑air PHY frame. Together they enable reliable, high‑performance wireless communication across modern Wi‑Fi networks.
