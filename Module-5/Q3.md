# Benefits of Target Wake Time (TWT) in Wi-Fi 6 for IoT Devices

## Introduction

Target Wake Time (TWT) is a major power-saving feature introduced in Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax).

It allows wireless devices to negotiate scheduled wake and sleep times with the Access Point (AP).

TWT is especially important for:
- IoT devices
- Battery-powered sensors
- Smart home devices
- Industrial wireless systems

---

# Problem Before TWT

In older Wi-Fi standards:
- Devices continuously contend for airtime
- Devices frequently wake to check traffic

Consequences:
- Higher power consumption
- Increased contention
- Reduced battery life

This was inefficient for low-power IoT devices.

---

# What is TWT?

TWT allows a client and AP to agree on:
- When the device should wake
- When it should sleep
- How often communication occurs

The device remains asleep outside scheduled periods.

---

# Basic Operation

## Step 1
Client requests TWT schedule.

## Step 2
AP negotiates wake interval.

## Step 3
Client sleeps most of the time.

## Step 4
Client wakes only during scheduled periods.

---

# Benefits of TWT

## 1. Improved Battery Life

This is the biggest advantage.

Devices avoid:
- Constant channel monitoring
- Frequent wake-ups

Battery life improves significantly.

---

## 2. Reduced Airtime Contention

Not all devices compete simultaneously.

Benefits:
- Reduced collisions
- Better scheduling efficiency
- Improved network scalability

---

## 3. Better IoT Scalability

Large IoT deployments may contain:
- Hundreds
- Thousands
of devices.

TWT enables efficient management of these devices.

---

## 4. Predictable Communication

Devices communicate at scheduled intervals.

Useful for:
- Industrial automation
- Sensor networks
- Smart metering

---

## 5. Reduced Network Congestion

By scheduling device activity:
- Random medium access reduces
- Congestion decreases

---

# Types of TWT

## Individual TWT
Negotiated between AP and single client.

## Broadcast TWT
Shared schedule for multiple devices.

Useful in massive IoT deployments.

---

# IoT Relevance

IoT devices typically:
- Send small periodic packets
- Operate on batteries
- Require long operational lifetime

TWT perfectly matches these requirements.

---

# Example Scenario

Smart temperature sensor:
- Needs to transmit once every 10 minutes

Without TWT:
- Device wakes frequently

With TWT:
- Device sleeps most of the time
- Wakes only during scheduled transmission time
