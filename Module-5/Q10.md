# How 802.11k, 802.11v, and 802.11r Work Together for Seamless Roaming

## Introduction

802.11k, 802.11v, and 802.11r are complementary Wi-Fi standards that collectively improve roaming performance in enterprise wireless networks.

Together they provide:
- Faster roaming
- Better roaming decisions
- Reduced latency
- Improved user experience

These standards are essential for:
- Voice-over-Wi-Fi
- Real-time applications
- High-mobility enterprise environments

---

# Problem in Traditional Roaming

Without roaming enhancements:
- Clients scan all channels
- Roaming decisions are inefficient
- Authentication delays occur

Consequences:
- Sticky clients
- Packet loss
- Voice interruptions
- Poor mobility experience

---

# Role of 802.11k

## Purpose
Neighbor discovery and radio measurements.

## Contribution to Roaming
The AP provides:
- Neighbor reports
- Recommended AP list
- Channel information

Benefits:
- Faster scanning
- Reduced roaming delay

---

# Role of 802.11v

## Purpose
Network-assisted roaming and client steering.

## Contribution to Roaming
The AP suggests:
- Better APs
- Load balancing decisions
- Roaming targets

Benefits:
- Smarter roaming decisions
- Improved load distribution

---

# Role of 802.11r

## Purpose
Fast secure transition between APs.

## Contribution to Roaming
802.11r:
- Pre-establishes roaming security context
- Reduces authentication overhead

Benefits:
- Very fast handoff
- Minimal communication interruption

---

# How They Work Together

## Step 1: Neighbor Discovery (802.11k)

Client receives neighbor AP information.

Result:
- Faster AP discovery

---

## Step 2: Network Guidance (802.11v)

Network recommends optimal AP.

Result:
- Better roaming decision

---

## Step 3: Fast Authentication (802.11r)

Client transitions quickly using pre-derived keys.

Result:
- Minimal roaming latency

---

# Combined Roaming Flow

1. Client signal weakens
2. AP sends neighbor recommendations (11k)
3. Network suggests best AP (11v)
4. Client performs fast transition (11r)
5. Session continues with minimal interruption

---

# Benefits in Enterprise Networks

## 1. Seamless Mobility
Users move without noticeable disruptions.

## 2. Better Voice Performance
VoWiFi calls remain stable.

## 3. Lower Roaming Delay
Fast AP transitions reduce latency.

## 4. Improved Load Balancing
Clients distribute more intelligently across APs.

---

# Real-World Example

In a hospital:
- Doctors move between rooms while using voice/video applications.

Using 11k/v/r:
- Roaming occurs quickly and efficiently
- Sessions remain stable

|---|---|

# Key Takeaways

- 802.11k, v, and r are complementary roaming technologies.
- 802.11k improves AP discovery.
- 802.11v improves roaming decisions.
- 802.11r reduces authentication delay.
- Together they enable seamless enterprise Wi-Fi roaming.
