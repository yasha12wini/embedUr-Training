# Purpose of 802.11k and 802.11v and Their Role in Roaming

## Introduction

802.11k and 802.11v are Wi-Fi standards designed to improve client mobility and roaming efficiency in wireless networks.

They help clients make better roaming decisions and improve overall WLAN performance.

These standards are especially important in:
- Enterprise networks
- Campus environments
- Voice-over-Wi-Fi deployments

---

# Problem in Traditional Roaming

Without roaming assistance:
- Clients independently decide when and where to roam
- Roaming may be slow or inefficient

Consequences:
- Sticky clients
- High latency
- Packet loss
- Poor voice/video performance

---

# What is 802.11k?

802.11k provides:
- Radio Resource Management (RRM)

It helps clients discover neighboring APs efficiently.

---

# Key Function of 802.11k

## Neighbor Reports

The AP provides a list of nearby APs including:
- Channel information
- BSSID
- Operating parameters

Benefits:
- Faster scanning
- Reduced roaming delay
- Lower power consumption

---

# Roaming Improvement with 802.11k

Without 802.11k:
- Client scans all channels

With 802.11k:
- Client scans only recommended APs

This significantly reduces roaming time.

---

# What is 802.11v?

802.11v provides:
- Wireless Network Management capabilities

It allows the network to guide client behavior.

---

# Key Functions of 802.11v

## BSS Transition Management

The AP can recommend:
- Better APs
- Better channels
- Load balancing decisions

---

## Power Management Improvements

802.11v also helps optimize:
- Client power-saving behavior

---

# How 802.11v Helps Roaming

The AP can suggest:
- When to roam
- Where to roam

This improves:
- Load balancing
- User experience
- Roaming efficiency

---

# 802.11k vs 802.11v

| Feature | 802.11k | 802.11v |
|---|---|---|
| Main Purpose | Neighbor discovery | Network-assisted roaming |
| Focus | Radio measurements | Client steering |
| Helps With | Faster scanning | Better AP selection |

---

# Combined Benefits

Together, they provide:
- Faster roaming
- Better roaming decisions
- Reduced scanning overhead
- Improved mobility performance

---

# Enterprise Importance

These standards are critical for:
- VoWiFi
- Real-time applications
- Large enterprise WLANs
- Mobility-intensive deployments

---

# Key Takeaways

- 802.11k helps clients discover nearby APs efficiently.
- 802.11v helps networks guide client roaming decisions.
- Together they improve roaming speed and wireless performance.
