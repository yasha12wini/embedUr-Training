# Major Innovations Introduced in Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be)

## Introduction

Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be), also called Extremely High Throughput (EHT), is designed to provide:
- Ultra-high throughput
- Very low latency
- Improved reliability
- Deterministic wireless performance

It is intended for:
- 8K streaming
- XR/VR
- Cloud gaming
- AI workloads
- Industrial real-time systems

---

# Major Innovations in Wi-Fi 7

## 1. Multi-Link Operation (MLO)

MLO allows devices to:
- Simultaneously use multiple frequency bands

Example:
- 5 GHz + 6 GHz concurrently

Benefits:
- Higher throughput
- Lower latency
- Better reliability

---

## 2. 320 MHz Channel Width

Wi-Fi 7 doubles the maximum channel width from:
- 160 MHz → 320 MHz

Benefits:
- Massive bandwidth increase
- Higher PHY rates

---

## 3. 4096-QAM

Wi-Fi 7 increases modulation density.

Comparison:
- Wi-Fi 6: 1024-QAM
- Wi-Fi 7: 4096-QAM

Benefits:
- Increased spectral efficiency
- Higher throughput

---

## 4. Multi-RU Allocation

Single clients can receive:
- Multiple Resource Units simultaneously

Benefits:
- Better scheduling flexibility
- Improved efficiency

---

## 5. Enhanced OFDMA

Wi-Fi 7 improves OFDMA efficiency through:
- Better scheduling
- Multi-RU usage
- Improved latency handling

---

## 6. Preamble Puncturing Improvements

Allows partial channel usage even when interference exists.

Benefits:
- Better channel utilization
- Reduced wasted bandwidth

---

## 7. Lower Latency Enhancements

Wi-Fi 7 introduces:
- Better scheduling
- Faster coordination
- MLO-based load balancing

Useful for:
- Gaming
- Real-time applications

---

# Throughput Improvements

Wi-Fi 7 can theoretically achieve:
- Extremely high data rates exceeding 30 Gbps under ideal conditions

This is a major leap compared to previous generations.

# Comparison with Wi-Fi 6

| Feature | Wi-Fi 6 | Wi-Fi 7 |
|---|---|---|
| Max Channel Width | 160 MHz | 320 MHz |
| Max QAM | 1024-QAM | 4096-QAM |
| MLO | No | Yes |
| Multi-RU | Limited | Advanced |
| Latency | Low | Ultra-Low |

---

# Enterprise Importance

Wi-Fi 7 is designed for future wireless demands involving:
- Massive bandwidth
- Real-time applications
- Dense client environments

It moves Wi-Fi closer to deterministic networking.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Wi-Fi 7 introduces major PHY and MAC improvements.
- MLO is one of the most important innovations.
- 320 MHz channels and 4096-QAM greatly increase throughput.
- Wi-Fi 7 targets ultra-low latency and high-capacity networking.
